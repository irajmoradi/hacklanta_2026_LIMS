# WEB-054 — "Around the village" feed: clickable, cleaner, readable (R-157, Mario)

Mario: (1) "If it says 'Iraj competition entry submitted', I should be able to click it to be redirected to whatever
competition entry he submitted." (2) "The 'See their accomplishments' button should be removed. It adds too much
repetitive text." (3) "Some messages are too long and get cut off. Put the long part as a subtitle, e.g. 'Maya made your
villager say: …' → title 'Maya made your villager say something!', subtitle = what they said."
1. **Deep links:** every feed row is a button that opens the relevant thing, using each event's payload ids (check
   `activityData.ts`/the API payloads): competition entry → the Colosseum on that competition with the entry selected;
   check-in/progress/evidence → that person's profile Activity tab scrolled to the check-in (evidence photo visible); goal
   events/stretch → their goal; purchase → the shop item; house/furniture events → their house (or the Hall); villager-gram or
   voice line → play it; reading → the reading list item; Mayor/Gazette/disaster → the Mayor, the Gazette or the alerts.
   Fall back to the person's profile. Keyboard accessible, with a clear hover affordance (chevron).
2. **Remove** the "See their accomplishments" button from feed rows (keep Accomplishments reachable from the profile/dashboard).
3. **Title + subtitle:** a short, never-truncated title per event type (e.g. "Maya made your villager say something!", "Iraj
   entered *Minecraft hours*", "Lance bought 2 things") and a one-line subtitle with the detail (quoted text, item names,
   competition title), ellipsized with a full tooltip. Grouped rows keep working. Keep the inline evidence photo (R-154).
Keep the plain voice (COPY-001). Unit tests (title/subtitle mapping for every event type; deep-link targets), plus a Playwright
check at 1440 and 390 px via `ops/remote_run.sh` (AGENTS.md); mock mode only. Final report per AGENTS.md.
