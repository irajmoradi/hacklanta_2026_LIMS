# VFX-001 — Beautiful, cinematic disasters

FEAT-012's disaster visuals (find them: grep Disaster in apps/web/src/scene + the disaster assets) are crude. Rebuild each as a stylized,
cozy-but-dramatic effect that matches the village art style (soft low-poly, warm palette, no gore, no harsh gray boxes). Trigger them with
the 🐞 debug menu to review.
- 🌋 **Meteor/volcano scorch:** a short intro (a glowing meteor streak with a trail, a camera shake ≤300 ms, a bloom flash, a ring shockwave
  on the ground), then a smoldering stylized crater: dark glassy rim, glowing orange cracks (emissive, gently pulsing), rising ember particles,
  soft smoke wisps (billboard sprites, alpha-faded), scorch decals. The affected building gets a charred tint and a cute "closed for
  repairs" wooden barricade with a sign; repair progress shows as scaffolding growing.
- 🐾 **Pet outbreak:** a vet van with a blinking soft light, a "Vet visit" banner, little cones, empty shelter yard with paw-print decals; pets
  that "ran away" become faint glowing paw trails leading out of town. Revival potion: sparkle burst + heart particles when a pet returns.
- 🌩️ **Storm blackout:** dramatic but pretty: drifting clouds, rain streaks (instanced lines), puddle ripples, occasional soft lightning
  flash lighting the town (≤2 per 10 s, no strobe; respect reduced motion), streetlights flicker then go dark; windows candle-lit.
- 🐛 **Garden blight:** gardens desaturate to dry tan, small cartoon bugs crawling, little sad wilting animation, a gentle dust haze.
- **Repair celebration:** confetti, sparkles, a quick "rebuilt" time-lapse, a chime.
Performance: instanced particles, ≤1 extra draw call per effect type, 60 fps on the 5090 and graceful on the low quality tier.
Short webm per disaster + before/after stills. Tests green. Final report per AGENTS.md.
