# WEB-038 — Try on before you buy

The Wardrobe has a live preview for owned items (`previewPerson` in Wardrobe.tsx), but shop items (regular shop + FEAT-013 shop/farm interiors)
can't be tried on. Add **👁 Try on** to every wearable/pet/house-addon card (not consumables): it shows YOUR villager wearing the item (merged with
your current loadout via `replaceSlot`, respecting FIX-008/012 fit rules and per-item colors) in a preview pane. In the walk-in clothing shop,
your villager steps in front of the tall mirror and the mirror shows the outfit; in the pet store, the pet sits beside you. A color row lets
you try colors before buying (FEAT-006 palettes). Buttons: Buy (price) / Done. Nothing is saved until you buy; house add-ons preview on your
house thumbnail. Keyboard + mobile friendly. Tests: unit (preview loadout merge), e2e (try on → preview shows item → buy → equipped),
screenshots. Final report.
