# FIX-004 — Cosmetics attach to the wrong place on villagers with a generated 3D head

Bug: on villagers whose manifest has `head_glb_url` (Hunyuan head on villager_base; Iraj, Lance, Stefanny, Mario), equipped
head cosmetics (glasses_round, hat_headphones, and other head/face slot items) render at the OLD base-head anchor: glasses at
chest height, and the headphones as a large teal block around the neck/shoulders. Expected: cosmetics sit on the generated
head (glasses on the eyes, headphones over the ears, hats on top), scaled to that head.
Fix in the attachment system (catalog attach anchors: head bone + offsets):
- When a generated head is present, compute the head anchor from that head's bounding box (top, eye line ≈ 0.55 of height,
  ear width) after it loads, and re-parent or offset head/face-slot attachments relative to it (scale by head width vs the base
  head's width).
- Hide the base head mesh if it's still rendering under the generated head.
- Make sure the headphones asset uses its own model scale (not a fallback Box at the wrong size).
Apply this in the town scene, the profile showcase/Outfit preview and the Wardrobe. Real-mode screenshot of Iraj (glasses +
headphones) and Stefanny from front and side. Unit test for anchor computation. Final report per AGENTS.md.
