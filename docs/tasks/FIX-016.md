# FIX-016 — Chorus / talking-head UI that looks good and is in sync

From the screenshot ("The village chorus" modal, FEAT-017 HeadStudio): a plain 4+1 card grid of circular photos; Stefanny's Gaussian head is a blank
box with Reset/⤢ buttons floating over it while it loads; "Voice permission needed" cards dominate; the line picker is a bare select; and the audio
starts before the heads are ready, so the lip-sync is missed.
1. **Sync:** never start audio until the speaking head(s) are loaded and the first frame is rendered. Preload Gaussian packages when the panel opens
   (show a skeleton shimmer + "Warming up faces…" progress), keep loaded heads cached across opens, and for the chorus wait for all consenting heads
   (or a 6 s timeout → speak with whichever are ready, others show their static portrait with a waveform). Audio + blendshapes start on the same
   clock (shared AudioContext time), so lips are in sync even after a slow load.
2. **Design:** a dark "stage" with a spotlight: consenting heads large in a row on the stage (only people who allowed voice), the active speaker
   scales up slightly with a soft glow and a subtitle line under them (karaoke-style word highlight), others idle (blink/breathe). People without
   permission go in a small, quiet "Not in the chorus (voice off)" strip below, not as full cards. The line picker becomes chips of suggested lines
   + a text field; primary buttons "Play" / "Everyone says it" / "Surprise me" with the custom icon set (WEB-037), no emoji.
3. Hide head controls (Reset/⤢) until hover, and never render them over an unloaded head.
4. Placeholder faces: the demo cast for Maya (and any `data/inputs/demo_cast` CC0 photo) must show the "placeholder face" label (per
   data/inputs/demo_cast/SOURCES.md), and placeholder faces never speak in AI voice.
Same improvements in the profile 🎤 panel. Tests: unit (start gating), e2e (mock slow head load → audio starts only after ready), screenshots +
a webm. Final report.
