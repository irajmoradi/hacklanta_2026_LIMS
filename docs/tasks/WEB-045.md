# WEB-045 — Walking: name tag off your body, your avatar highlighted (R-116, owner)

Owner: "if I start walking my nametag should stop covering my body and body should be highlighted right?"
When the current user is controlling/walking their own avatar (playerControl / walk mode / follow):
1. **Name tag:** your own tag must not cover your body — shrink it to a compact pill (name only, no activity row), lift it
   clearly above the head (offset by the avatar's measured head height, not a constant), and fade it while moving;
   restore on stop/exit. Other villagers' tags must not overlap your avatar either (declutter/fade tags that intersect it).
2. **Highlight:** your avatar gets a clear, tasteful highlight while walking — e.g. a soft outline/rim (drei `<Outlines>` or an
   inverted-hull pass on the avatar meshes) plus a ground ring/shadow disc under your feet in the accent color; visible
   against grass, roads, night mode. Off when not walking (or subtle). Must not cost meaningful fps (no full-screen
   postprocessing pass).
