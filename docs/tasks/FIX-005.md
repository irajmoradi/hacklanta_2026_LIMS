# FIX-005 — Cosmetics now ride on the 3D head but fit badly

After FIX-004, side view of Iraj (generated Hunyuan head + glasses_round + hat_headphones):
1. The **headphones** render as a huge teal bucket that swallows the top of the head (wrong scale and orientation: the band should
   arc over the crown, the cups on the ears).
2. The **glasses** sit too far forward, poking out in front of the face instead of on the nose and eyes.
3. The head floats slightly above the body (neck gap or shift) and looks oversized relative to the torso.
Fix the fitting logic for generated heads: derive a head frame from the loaded head GLB (bbox center, front direction = +Z of
the head, eye line, ear points at ±x on the bbox at ~0.5 height). Per-slot fit rules: glasses = eye line, depth at the face
front, minus a small inset, width = 0.9 × face width; headphones and hats = scale to head width (cups at the ear points, band
over the crown); hats = sit on top with a slight forward tilt. Add optional per-item `fit` overrides in the catalog (offset/scale
per slot for generated heads). Close the neck gap (snap the head bottom to the body's neck anchor) and normalize the head to
~1.05× the base head height. Verify for all four real users (Iraj, Lance, Stefanny, Mario) with every head-slot cosmetic in
the catalog: front, side and back screenshots. Unit tests for the fit math. Final report per AGENTS.md.
