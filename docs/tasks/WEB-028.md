# WEB-028 — The Colosseum IS the Compete screen

Today: Compete opens a list panel, and the colosseum (FEAT-005: components/Colosseum.tsx, scene/ColosseumScene.tsx) appears
only after you click a competition. Make it Colosseum-first:
1. **Compete → straight into the arena** (≤300 ms transition: quick camera swoop/fade from town to arena, then town rendering
   paused). The empty arena idles with ambience (torches, dust, crowd murmur respecting the sound toggle).
2. **In-arena HUD** replaces the list panel: a left rail of competition "banners" (title, mode, status chip open/active/settled,
   participant avatars stack, pot/fee), filter tabs **Open · Current · Mine · Past**, and a big **+ Create** banner. Hovering a
   banner previews it: its participants pop into the arena (open = at the gate, current = by rank, top 3 on the podium) with
   staggered spring-in animations. Clicking locks it in and shows the terms card with the ominous Join (cost confirm + drum) or
   your standing. Clicking a person zooms to their evidence card (existing). The Create flow is a stylized scroll/plaque form
   inside the arena, not a separate panel; on create, the new banner unfurls and its arena view loads.
3. **Snappier and nicer animations:** spring-based UI transitions (≤180 ms), banner unfurl, avatars dropping in with a dust puff,
   camera moves with ease-out cubic under 450 ms, no layout jank, 60 fps on a laptop (instance and reuse avatars, no re-mount on
   filter change). Respect reduced-motion (fades only).
4. **Back to village:** Esc or a "← Village" button returns with a reverse swoop, restoring the town camera.
Keep all existing competition logic and API calls (join/entries/decision/settle, SOL intent copy, auditor rules). Tests: e2e
(Compete opens arena; create from arena; join confirm; filter tabs; person → evidence), a perf check (no frame >50 ms during
transitions in the Playwright trace), real screenshots and a short screen recording (webm). Final report per AGENTS.md.
